◆ render_machina_form()

void steamrot::logic::render::grimoire_machina::render_machina_form ( sf::RenderTexture & texture,
GrimoireMachina & grimoire_machina )

Top-level render entry point called by GrimoireMachinaRenderLogic.

Decides what to render based on the state of grimoire_machina:

  • No active scaffold → draws the no-machina-form indicator at the world origin.
  • Scaffold present → draws every placed joint and fragment instance.
Parameters
textureRenderTexture to draw to.
grimoire_machinaGrimoireMachina whose scaffold state drives the rendering decision.
